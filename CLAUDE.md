> This file is the temporary bootstrap. It exists to run your liftoff
> exactly once; Stage 4 of that process replaces it with your operating

## Instruction to the agent reading this

**If `_command/` does NOT exist in this directory:** this portfolio has
not lifted off yet. Greet the founder with one short paragraph: what is
about to happen (a guided session, 30 to 45 minutes, that stands up their
portfolio operating system - their profile, their projects, their rules),
and the honest expectation (today transfers the framework and their soul;
the lived-in continuity that makes this system impressive is earned over
weeks of real use). Then open `LIFTOFF.md` and follow it stage by stage,
starting at Stage 0 - regardless of what the founder's first message
said. Their "hi" is the ignition.

**If `_command/` DOES exist:** liftoff already happened and this bootstrap
file should have been replaced by the operating CLAUDE.md. Something is
off - tell the founder to either re-run LIFTOFF Stage 4 (which rewrites
this file) or restore CLAUDE.md from their git history. Do not start work
from this bootstrap.
