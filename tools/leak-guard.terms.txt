# leak-guard deny-list - process vocabulary that must not reach an outward artifact
#
# One case-insensitive REGEX per line. Blank lines and lines starting with #
# are ignored. Every term is matched against the tool input only, never against
# the repo's files.
#
# TUNING RULE: prefer a multiword phrase over a bare word. A bare common word
# is a false-positive engine: "gate" is inside delegate, mitigate, aggregate,
# navigate, investigate, and "gateway" is ordinary code. Every entry below is
# either multiword or a word nobody writes by accident in a commit message.
#
# SCOPE WARNING: a repo whose SUBJECT is process (this one) will legitimately
# use these words in its commits. Enable the full list only on repos where the
# words are leakage, not subject matter. See framework/leak-guard.md.

# --- attribution of a decision to a person rather than to the change ---
\bfounder (said|says|decided|asked|wants|wanted|approved|confirmed|rejected)\b
\bper the founder\b
\bas the founder (asked|requested|instructed)\b
\bthe founder's (call|decision|request)\b
\bthe owner (decided|locked)\b
\bas (instructed|requested) by\b

# --- internal orchestration vocabulary ---
# NOT bare \borchestrator\b or \bsub-?agents?\b. Both are ordinary domain
# nouns: a Saga orchestrator is a microservices pattern, and an SNMP AgentX
# sub-agent is RFC 2741 vocabulary. Blocked on 2026-08-13 in review against
# "add the orchestrator service to coordinate payment steps" and "restart the
# AgentX sub-agent on socket timeout", both legitimate. Bind them to the verb
# that makes them process talk.
\bthe orchestrator (decided|dispatched|integrated|held|holds)\b
\b(dispatch(ed|es)?|brief(ed)?) (a |the )?sub-?agents?\b
# NOT \bpause point\b. "insert a pause point at the marker" is ordinary video
# editing vocabulary, and ytkit is a video tool. The phrase is also obsolete:
# MC-006 removed pause points from the flow.
\bthe gate (passed|failed)\b
\bgate-locked\b
\bfan-?out budget\b

# --- authorship tells ---
